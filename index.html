DAY 2 · LESSON 2C · INTRODUCTION

Gross versus net profit

Gross P&L is the selling value minus the buying value, before trading charges. Net P&L subtracts applicable charges from gross P&L.

Example: buy 10 shares at ₹500 and sell at ₹600. Gross profit = ₹1,000. If total charges are ₹35, net profit = ₹965.

This is a practice example, not a complete lesson or verified assessment. Trading charges depend on market, product, broker and applicable rules.
