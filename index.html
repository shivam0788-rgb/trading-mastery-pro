Lesson 2C: Trading costs

Gross P&L = (sell price − buy price) × quantity for a completed long equity trade. Net P&L = gross P&L − all applicable charges.

Example: 10 shares bought at ₹500 and sold at ₹600 gives ₹1,000 gross profit. If total charges are ₹35, net profit is ₹965.

Costs can include brokerage, STT, exchange transaction charges, SEBI fees, stamp duty, GST and other applicable levies. They depend on product, exchange, broker and current rules; this app does not guess their rates.

Privacy and limitations

GitHub Pages hosts a public website. Your journal and notes are saved in this app's local browser storage, not committed to GitHub. This is not encrypted storage; anyone with access to your unlocked device/browser profile may access it. No automatic ChatGPT synchronization, live market data or conversational AI service.
